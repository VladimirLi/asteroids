Perihelion Golf privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

Perihelion Golf does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device and never sends data over the internet.

What is stored, and where

Your game progress (stars, best scores, Daily Orbit results, logbook entries) and your settings (music volume, sound volume, vibration) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them.

You can delete this data at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

What is not collected

No account, name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash reports. The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics code.

Permissions

Vibration provides haptic feedback during play; you can turn it off in Settings. The standard Android network permission is present only because the game engine displays its screens through Android's built-in web view; the game makes no network requests.

Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.
