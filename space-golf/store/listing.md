## Full description (max 4000)
Long ago, someone built a golf course across an entire star system. Its sun has burned low and the cups have gone dark. You are the last groundskeeper. Sink a ball and a cup lights up again. Light them all and the sun might wake.

Perihelion Golf is mini-golf where gravity does the bending. Pull back, aim and let go, then watch planets curve your shot, black holes try to swallow it and wormholes carry it across the course. The dotted preview line shows where the first moments of each shot will go, so every miss teaches you something.

36 HANDCRAFTED HOLES IN FOUR CHAPTERS
• First Light: planets, slingshots, moons and asteroids
• The Dark Rough: black holes and red repulsor stars
• Nebula Greens: braking clouds and wormholes
• The Pulsar Crown: pulsars that switch on and off, binary suns and a final Grand Tour

EVERY HOLE IS FAIR
Every hole has been checked by computer to make sure it can be holed in one. If it looks impossible, there is a way.

A NEW HOLE EVERY DAY
Daily Orbit builds a fresh hole each day. Everyone gets the same one, so you can compare scores with friends and keep your streak going. Free Orbit gives you endless random holes when you want one more.

MADE TO BE PLAYED
• Earn up to three stars on every hole
• 17 logbook entries to collect
• Music and sound generated live on your device
• Plays fully offline
• One-handed portrait play, or landscape
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
