## Description (paste into the page body)

Long ago, someone built a golf course across an entire star system. Its sun has burned low and the cups have gone dark. You are the last groundskeeper. Sink a ball and a cup lights up again. Light them all and the sun might wake.

**Perihelion Golf** is mini-golf where gravity does the bending. Pull back, aim and let go, then watch planets curve your shot, black holes try to swallow it and wormholes carry it across the course. The dotted preview shows only the start of each shot: the rest you have to read from the course.

**36 handcrafted holes in four chapters**
- First Light: planets, slingshots, moons and asteroids
- The Dark Rough: black holes and red repulsor stars
- Nebula Greens: braking clouds and wormholes
- The Pulsar Crown: pulsars that switch on and off, binary suns and a final Grand Tour

**Every hole is fair.** Each one has been checked by computer to make sure it can be holed in one. Stuck? After two misses the game can show you a line to copy.

**A new hole every day.** Daily Orbit builds a fresh hole each day, the same for everyone. Free Orbit gives you endless random holes.

**Shots worth showing off.** Land a hole in one and time slows as the ball drops in. Share any shot as an image of its actual path.

**Also**
- 9 balls and trails to unlock, up to three stars on every hole, 17 logbook entries
- Music and sound generated live in your browser
- Plays on phone and desktop, portrait or landscape
- No ads, no purchases, no tracking. Progress is saved in your browser.

**Controls:** drag anywhere and pull back like a slingshot, then let go. Mouse or touch.

Feedback is very welcome in the comments: which holes felt too easy, too hard, or unfair?
